Everything changed since {{ backup.created_at }} is lost: every row in every table, users and settings included. Backups, schedules and rollbacks themselves are kept.

  • The app goes down for maintenance while it runs. Everyone, you included, gets the maintenance page until it is done; requests in progress are cut off.
  • A backup of the database as it is now is taken first ("Before a rollback"), so this can be undone by rolling back to that.
  • The restore runs in one transaction: if it fails, nothing changes.
  • Uploaded files are not part of the database: files of uploads made since stay on disk, and files of uploads deleted since are not brought back.
